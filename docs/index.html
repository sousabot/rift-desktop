FAQ

Everything you need to know

How does the Premium giveaway work?

Follow @RIFT_LOL_ on X and retweet the official giveaway post. Winners are drawn from accounts that completed both steps. Details and dates are on the Giveaway section of this page.

What is Rift Premium?

Premium unlocks Rift Lens, Data Studio, and deeper insights on top of the free desktop app — dashboard, draft, overlays, spectate, and collections stay available on every plan.

Does the app work on Windows and Mac?

Rift.lol is Windows-only for now (Windows 10/11). A Mac build is not available yet.

Is the Rift app allowed by Riot?

Yes. Rift uses Riot’s official APIs and League’s local Live Client Data for overlays. It does not inject into the game client or automate play. Always follow Riot’s Terms of Service.

How do I install the app?

Download the Setup installer from this page, run it, then link your Riot ID (Name#TAG) in the app. If Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info, then Run anyway — the build is not code-signed yet.

How can I cancel my Premium subscription?

Cancel anytime from your Stripe customer portal or the Premium page in the app. You keep access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

How can I change my subscription tier?

Pick a different plan (1 month, 6 months, or 1 year) on the Premium page in the app. Upgrades and renewals are handled through checkout; you won’t be double-billed for unused time when Stripe is configured.

What is Rift Lens?

Lens is a Premium insight view across your recent games — fighting, laning, objectives, vision, survivability, and team impact, with sparklines and rank benchmarks.

Can I use Lens on more than one account?

Premium is tied to your device and the Riot ID you link in the app. You can switch the linked account, but each Premium seat is meant for one active player setup — not unlimited shared logins.

How do I link my Riot account?

Open Link Account in the app, enter your Riot Name and TAG, and verify. Stats, draft, and scout then load for that player on your PC.

Do overlays work in Exclusive Fullscreen?

No. Use Borderless or Windowed in League (Esc → Video). Exclusive Fullscreen cannot show a desktop HUD on top of the game.
